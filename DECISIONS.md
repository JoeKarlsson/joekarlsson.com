# Design decisions

Joe's standing verdicts for this site, one per line, dated. Read before any visual change; add each new verdict the moment Joe gives it. A line here outranks a mockup, a skill default or a design rule.

## Look

- 2026-11-05: No wavy or warping effects on the hero videos. Dithering is fine, varied and light.
- 2026-11-05: Dithering stays light: heavy dithering made the hero videos and blog thumbnails hard to see. 2026-11-10: "Less dithering, but I like the pink and black."
- 2026-11-06: Images and video get the site's pink color grading. Never drop it (2026-11-09: "why did you take the pink off of it?").
- 2026-11-05: No photo board with stickers on the About page.
- 2026-11-10: The resume page uses a still image, not a video clip.
- 2026-11-12: Recently shipped repo names always show in full, never cut off with an ellipsis. Long ones shrink to fit, down to 24px, then wrap at their hyphens.
- 2026-11-12: The resume PDF carries the site's brand on white paper (Anton, Hanken Grotesk, Fraunces, JetBrains Mono, pink rules and bullet markers), not pink on black: it gets printed and pasted into hiring systems.
- 2026-11-11: No date in the corner of blog post or newsletter thumbnails.
- 2026-11-14: Art book layout round (~/mockups/joekarlsson-art-book, 10 ideas): Joe picked 5 (New Wave collision: overlapping stepped Anton lines, pink slab, tilted picture, giant punctuation), 8 (chapter openers: giant outlined numeral, Fraunces italic title, an epigraph from the post), 7 (monograph margins: sources and captions in a wide outer margin beside the line they belong to) and 10 (section name running up the edge like a book spine, a colophon and page number at the end of the page). Passed on exhibition plates, Swiss grid, open spread, type specimen, contents page and photo book sequence. Not built yet; round 2 decides where each one goes.
- 2026-11-14: Art book round 2, Joe: "1b, 5a, 8b, 7b, 10a". Post page as mocked in 1b. 5A: the New Wave collision is the homepage hero only (the featured post); post headers keep the cut-paper PaperHero. 8B: chapter openers (outlined numeral, Fraunces italic title, an exact quote from the post as epigraph) at the big sections of long posts only, no full-screen opener before a post. 7B: "On this page" moves into the right margin with the sidenotes and captions, leaving the left edge to the spine. 10A: the spine runs on every page, posts included, and every page ends with a colophon and page number.

Pages in the post layout (posts, reviews, talks) share one header style. The New Wave collision as that header (round 3, ~/mockups/joekarlsson-art-book/round3.html) was rejected: "I don't think I like this." Three more header ideas next.
- 2026-11-15: Post-layout pages (posts, newsletter issues, reviews, talks) get the masthead header (round 4 option C, ~/mockups/joekarlsson-art-book/round4.html), picked over a torn title strip and a catalog card: a kicker line between a thick pink rule and a thin one, the title big and fitted to the column, then the picture with no tilt or tape and an italic caption carrying the description.
- 2026-11-15: Margin notes come from real citations and footnotes added by hand (round 5 option B, ~/mockups/joekarlsson-art-book/round5.html), not a note on every outside link: product and docs links stay plain. A citation's note gives author, date, title and site. Start with the posts that quote or argue with other writers; short posts with no sources keep an empty margin.
- 2026-11-15: Margin notes go beyond citations (round 6, ~/mockups/joekarlsson-art-book/round6.html): Joe approved terms, see also, updates, exhibits and sources, and asides when the facts exist ("asides are fine if we have those"). His one problem: "It's hard to tell like what section it's related to." A note must link to the words it belongs to, so clicking it takes him there, "kind of like we have on the uses page" (numbered pins that light and circle their entry).

- 2026-11-15: Review page design review (live /reviews/the-little-hours/): Joe approved every fix ("they all look good") and asked for a mockup first (~/mockups/joekarlsson-review-prestige): split the stitched two-frame pairs, square-cornered captioned frames with more air, a credits block from the front matter, the review's axis lined up, More reviews right after the review, a captioned hero, plus the lightbox mid-open close, poster-lift reduced motion and arrow pause fixes. - 2026-11-15: Pictures and clips in a post's text sit at the text-column width on every page that has them (blog posts, newsletter issues, reviews), like a blog post with an "On this page" column (Joe: "my blog page is like the de facto standard for how I want them displayed"); nothing breaks out to the hero's width any more. - 2026-11-15: No paste-down animation on a post's or review's first photo: it animated only one image per page, which Joe found annoying. Overrides the 2026-11-14 "one paste-down photo per post".

- 2026-11-13: Hovering a spoiler shows just that one; clicking or tapping any spoiler reveals every spoiler in the review. The spoiler warning is "Contains spoilers" in the meta line only: no explainer note telling readers how to reveal them (Joe: "way too verbose", AI slop).
- 2026-11-13: The /reviews/stats/ page combines the dashboard and poster mockups: the dashboard's top numbers bar, then its Ratings and By month charts, then the poster-built stats (every review as a poster tile by rating, decades, directors, five stars).
- 2026-11-13: Posters on the stats page enlarge on hover and show the film's one-line review (the description); clicking one opens the full review.

- 2026-11-13: Stats page motion, all from the site's kit: crayon notes that draw once on the charts, cut-paper bar edges that boil on hover, a dock-style magnifier on the rating wall, Ratings bars that light their wall row, a popcorn-eating stick figure on a bar, KPI numbers that slot-roll on hover, posters that peel like stickers with a pink crayon star on five-star films.

- 2026-11-14: A rating Joe gave always shows as star glyphs (★★★½), on the stats page as everywhere else, including under director posters and as the Ratings chart's labels (option A, stacked on phones, over Letterboxd-style stars at the two ends only). ## Consistency

- 2026-11-13: /reviews (Joe's film reviews): the list is a poster wall, and each review page looks exactly like a blog post page. Every page keeps the site's own header; a mockup that left it out read as "a header different than any of my other pages". Reviews stays out of the nav ("no one really cares"): it's linked from the About page and the footer, and since 2026-11-14 no nav item lights up on a review page (About used to, which didn't match what Joe was looking at), and the About page's recent movies link to their reviews. A review ends with links to more of Joe's reviews.
- 2026-11-13: Media in a review is a frame that supports the argument: the specific moment the paragraph is making a point about, grabbed from the film itself. Never generic posters or backdrops, and not a picture for every film the review mentions ("You're just grabbing like generic posters... This is like a frame that supports my argument").
- 2026-11-13: Frames in a review stay in natural color, an exception to the pink grading rule: the color is often the point (the Odyssey review's "sandy skin" Cyclops). Picked B, natural, over A, pink graded.
- 2026-11-13: When the line spoken in a review frame is the point, the subtitle is burned into the frame (white, black outline, bottom center, the film's own subtitle wording), and the caption under it doesn't repeat the quote. Review media never gets pink, black or dithering: it looks like a picture in a blog post.
- 2026-11-14: A review frame that shows an ending or a death is a spoiler too: it stays blurred under a "Show spoiler" label until a click or tap, which shows every spoiler in the review (Joe: "you can implement the blur for frames for spoiler images"). Hover doesn't show a picture.

- 2026-11-05: Consistency across the site comes first. Same column width for a post's text, the footer and the about/keep-reading blocks. Same hero treatment and video formatting on every page. The 404 page matches the rest (2026-11-10).
- 2026-11-10: The bottom of each page shows a next step that fits that page (a featured item worth pushing there), not one list everywhere. The contact page links to no posts.

## Images of Joe

Pick frames for that, not for "clarity". Profile photos and headshots are different: no smiling-at-camera shots (memory feedback-headshots-no-smiling-camera-shots).

## Animation

- 2026-11-14: Every chart on the site builds in as it scrolls into view (not just boils), including all of the sentence-length post's charts. Overrides the 2026-11-13 motion review that left only that post's Dickens chart animating. No overshoot past a data value.
- 2026-11-13: A post's animated figures stay at their empty first frame until they're mostly on screen, so the reader sees each one build from nothing. That's intended, not a blank-figure bug (a motion review flagged it).
- 2026-11-13: Subscribing plays a cut-paper envelope that seals with the crayon asterisk (picked over a rubber "SENT" stamp). It plays on listmonk's "check your inbox" page, not on the site's form, so it never holds up the hand-off and plays in full (Joe's call).

- 2026-11-10: Animations should think bigger than redrawing the post's diagrams: stick figures, drawings, the scribbly pink crayon style.
- 2026-11-13: Motion round 2 (motion.dev), all option A: the lightbox swipes down to close and pinches or double-taps to zoom (up to 4x, pan, rubber-band edges; a tap waits ~250ms for a double-tap); a blog thumbnail grows into the post's paper header across the page load (picture only, not the title); a post header's tape and topic scrap drag a short way and spring back with a small overshoot (not left where dropped); the picked drawing-board crayon rises and a mouse over the board draws with a dot in that color that pulses on a switch.
- 2026-11-13: Borrowed-motion round (ideas from motion.dev and bklit.com), all option A: hovering a day on a Now-feed commit strip fades the other days and the caption shows that day's count (no crayon ring per cell, too busy); a code block's Copy draws the loose 12-ray starburst around "Copied!" once, then fades (not the spiky star, which covers the code); Newest/Popular re-sort brings the rows in 30ms apart. Search results stay instant.
- 2026-11-14: Page transitions round, all recommended: Works and review posters grow into the project header and the review's poster slot across the page load, like the blog thumbnails; on every nav click the nav bar holds still, the pink current-page box slides to the new section and only the content changes (old out fast, new rises 8px), picked over today's whole-page fade, a crayon-scribble wipe (fun but too slow for every click) and a paper-sheet lift; Back plays each transition in reverse.
- 2026-11-14: Page transitions, second set: the page slides in the direction you're going, consistent on every page change (a section to the right of the current one in the nav comes in from the right, one to the left from the left, Back the opposite way); a crayon squiggle draws under the clicked nav link only when the next page takes longer than ~0.3s; talk posters and the home page's project video posters grow into their player or project page like the other poster morphs; the Keep exploring cards at a post's end turn the next post in like a page. Skipped: a crayon "you were here" circle on the card you came back to.
- 2026-11-14: New-CSS round (mockup ~/mockups/joekarlsson-css-features), all eight option B: scroll-driven reading line and one paste-down photo per post; hover link previews (taped card) for links to other posts; hand-pasted tilt (sibling-index) on card walls (Works, review posters, talk cards), not the blog list; corner-shape cut corners; shape() torn edges with a fixed-depth tear; text-box trim on Anton labels and buttons; CSS-only carousel arrows and dots; @property highlighter sweep. Joe: "you can do all of them" (including 4 and 7, which I'd marked maybe/skip).
- 2026-11-15: Kokonut UI round (mockup ~/mockups/joekarlsson-kokonut-ideas): rejected all three, page flip at chapter openers, a synced video transcript and a contact sheet for photo runs. Joe: "They don't feel like they fit. They look bad. I don't really get the purpose of them. They don't add anything and aren't very interesting." Don't port UI-library components; an idea has to come from what a page needs.
- 2026-11-14: No cut corners on the nav pills or buttons (`.sticker`): the beveled nav bar shipped in the new-CSS round got "I do not like the beveled nav bar revert that - YUCK"; notched ones read as 8-bit before that. They stay plain rounded.
