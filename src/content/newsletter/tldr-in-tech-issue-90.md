## [The Best Managers Don't Fix, They Coach - Four Tools To Add to Your Toolkit](https://web.archive.org/web/20230926115626/https://review.firstround.com/the-best-managers-dont-fix,-they-coach-four-tools-to-add-to-your-toolkit)

_By: First Round Review_

tl;dr: Learn how to coach - not "fix" - team members in various situations like: (1) Outcome shift: assisting a team member who is spinning on a problem. (2) Options exploration: understanding a team member's challenge and desired outcome. (3) Acknowledging strengths: helping team members overcome imposter syndrome or a lack of confidence. (4) Identifying unconscious assumptions that might be holding a team member back.

## [Two Types Of Software Engineers](https://registerspill.thorstenball.com/p/two-types-of-software-engineers)

_By: Thorsten Ball_

tl;dr: Thorsten presents two types of engineers: Type 1 thinks a problem is "easy, people can just do X," while Type 2 considers it "very hard, because it requires people to do X." Understanding this subtle difference is important in problem-solving.

## [Junior To Senior: An Action Plan For Engineering Career Success](https://github.com/readme/guides/engineering-career-success)

_By: Jerome Hardaway_

tl;dr: This guide covers: (1) Key technical competencies managers prioritize for career advancement. (2) Essential communication skills expected from senior developers. (3) How to approach code development with a focus on addressing business requirements.

## [Taming The Nested Beast](https://www.16elt.com/2023/03/24/nested-code-and-complexity/)

## [6 CSS Snippets Every Front-End Developer Should Know In 2023](https://web.dev/6-css-snippets-every-front-end-developer-should-know-in-2023/)

_By: Adam Argyle_

tl;dr: Handy CSS snippets for front-end developers include: (1) A container query. (2) Scroll snap. (3) Grid pile. (4) Quick circle. (5) Control variants with @layer. (6) Logical properties for memorizing less and reaching more.

## [Fast-Forwarding Decision Making](https://theengineeringmanager.substack.com/p/fast-forwarding-decision-making)

_By: James Stanier_

tl;dr: James advises to hold yourself accountable for making decisions and progressing discussions as quickly as possible. Dead time is your enemy, so be creative about ways to shave minutes, hours, and days from a decision point.

## [Slack](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/Slack.html)

_By: Martin Fowler_

tl;dr: Martin discusses the concept of "slack" in timeboxed iterations, where time isn't allocated for user stories but rather for unplanned work. Though it may seem inefficient, it often leads to significant improvements in a team's productivity.

## [Time Is Emphasis: Planning Your Calendar As A Leader](https://mollyg.substack.com/p/time-is-emphasis-planning-your-calendar)

_By: Molly Graham_

tl;dr: Molly shares insights from a study analyzing the calendars of 27 CEOs, highlighting the importance of having explicit priorities and structure for your calendar. She offers templates and examples to help you allocate time for strategic priorities effectively.

## [Real-time Messaging](https://slack.engineering/real-time-messaging/)

_By: Sameera Thangudu_

tl;dr: The engineering team at Slack takes us through the architecture used to send real-time messages at scale, focusing on the services that send chat messages and events to online users in real time.

## [Semantic Search In iMessage, iMessage Wrapped, And AI Conversations](https://blog.jonlu.ca/posts/mimessage)
