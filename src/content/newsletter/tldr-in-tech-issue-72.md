## 🚀 [Developer Advocacy in 2023](https://joekarlsson.com/2023/03/developer-advocacy-in-2023/) - _Joe Karlsson_

**tl;dr:** Techpocalypse, anyone? 🌋 I've got the lowdown on how developer advocacy can adapt and survive in these tumultuous times. Get ready for some serious internal collaboration, ROI magic, and proving that DevRel is more than just nerdy hype-machines.

## 🚫 [Retrospectives Antipatterns](https://martinfowler.com/articles/retrospective-antipatterns.html) - _Aino Corry_

**tl;dr:** Are your retrospectives more like _retro-spectacles_? 😅 Aino tackles common pitfalls that can make these meetings a total drag. Remember, folks: don't skip on insights, avoid wallowing in the unchangeable, and don't let loudmouths steal the show.

## 🍯 [How We Manage Incident Response At Honeycomb](https://thenewstack.io/how-we-manage-incident-response-at-honeycomb/) - _Fred Herbert_

**tl;dr:** Fred dishes on the sweet, sweet nectar of incident response at Honeycomb 🐝. Get the buzz on managing unknowns, cognitive bandwidth, coordination, psychological safety, and feeding info back into your organization. Don't let incidents sting you! 🐝

## 🎨 [The Art Of Knowing When To Quit](https://blog.jim-nielsen.com/2023/art-of-knowing-when-to-quit/) - _Jim Nielsen_

**tl;dr:** It ain't quittin' time yet, but Jim shows us how there's a fine art to knowing when to say "I'm done" in software engineering. Sometimes, being done is the best thing you can do! So, remember to draw the line, folks. 🖌️

## 📱 [Image Stacks And iPhone Racks - Building An Internet Scale Meme Search Engine](https://findthatmeme.com/blog/2023/01/08/image-stacks-and-iphone-racks-building-an-internet-scale-meme-search-engine-Qzrz7V6T.html) - _Matthew Bryant_

**tl;dr:** Are you tired of losing your meme game mid-conversation? 😢 Matthew was too, so he built an internet-scale meme search engine! This article is a meme-tastic journey into the world of niche humor and finding the perfect meme to send to your buddies. 😂

## 🏗️ [Writing Javascript Without A Build System](https://jvns.ca/blog/2023/02/16/writing-javascript-without-a-build-system/) - _Julia Evans_

**tl;dr:** Julia is kicking it old-school with JavaScript sans build systems. 🕺 Who needs 'em, anyway? Learn why some folks like Julia are still rocking simple, small projects without the extra baggage. Less is sometimes more! 💼

## 🌐 [A Gentle Introduction To CRDTs](https://vlcn.io/blog/gentle-intro-to-crdts.html)

**tl;dr:** Get a gentle intro to these magical data structures that guarantee eventual consistency across your networked applications. It's like a warm hug from your favorite distributed system. 🤗
