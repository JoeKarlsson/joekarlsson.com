[tl;dr: Murat committed to asking "crazier" left-field questions in each post he wrote. After 70 posts, he learned that these:](https://muratbuffalo.blogspot.com/2018/12/master-your-questioning-skills.html)

[1) Are useful as they took him out of "default mode."](https://muratbuffalo.blogspot.com/2018/12/master-your-questioning-skills.html)

[2) Keep the brain more engaged. We tend to ask easier questions. Harder ones feel uncomfortable or seem impolite.](https://muratbuffalo.blogspot.com/2018/12/master-your-questioning-skills.html)

[3) By calling them "MAD" questions, they give him the license to be more uninhibited.](https://muratbuffalo.blogspot.com/2018/12/master-your-questioning-skills.html)

[tl;dr:](https://medium.com/the-ready/how-to-eliminate-organizational-debt-8a949c06b61b)

[1) Launch a bounty program so any employee that encounters a policy or process hindering their ability to deliver value can submit it with a recommendation.](https://medium.com/the-ready/how-to-eliminate-organizational-debt-8a949c06b61b)

[2) Practice continuous governance so individuals have the power to recommend changes to their own roles.](https://medium.com/the-ready/how-to-eliminate-organizational-debt-8a949c06b61b)

[3) Don't rush to a policy and process for everything as there can be an efficiency cost to keeping things open-ended.](https://medium.com/the-ready/how-to-eliminate-organizational-debt-8a949c06b61b)
