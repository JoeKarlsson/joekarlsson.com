![Experiment: The hidden costs of waiting on slow build times](/images/newsletter/tldr-in-tech-issue-64/image-3.webp)

_**tl;dr**_: Senior Solutions Engineer at GitHub conducted an experiment to understand tradeoffs between productivity and hardware. "When you ask a developer whether they'd prefer more or less powerful hardware, the answer is almost always more powerful hardware. More powerful hardware means less time waiting on builds - and that means more time to build the next feature or fix a bug. But even if the upfront cost is higher for higher-powered hardware, what's the actual cost when you consider the impact on productivity?"

Via: [Experiment: The Hidden Costs Of Waiting On Slow Build Times](https://bit.ly/3WlCBsY) - Natalie Somersall
