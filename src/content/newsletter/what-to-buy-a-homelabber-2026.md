This week I published [a homelab gift guide](https://www.joekarlsson.com/blog/homelab-gift-ideas-2026/) built from the gear in my rack and on my desk.

![Six picks from the gift guide flipping by one at a time: Rackstuds, my mug warmer, the Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition, an Elgato Stream Deck, the WiiM Pro and the Aranet4 Home, each with its price](/images/newsletter/what-to-buy-a-homelabber-2026/picks.gif)

It's for software engineers, homelabbers and anyone who works at a computer all day.

- **[Rackstuds](https://www.joekarlsson.com/blog/homelab-gift-ideas-2026/#rackstuds)** (under $50): tool-free cage nut replacements. I love these.
- **[Mug warmer](https://www.joekarlsson.com/blog/homelab-gift-ideas-2026/#mug-warmer)** (under $50): the least technical thing on my desk.
- **[Home Assistant Voice PE](https://www.joekarlsson.com/blog/homelab-gift-ideas-2026/#home-assistant-voice-preview-edition)** (about $69): replaced every Alexa in my house.
- **[Elgato Stream Deck](https://www.joekarlsson.com/blog/homelab-gift-ideas-2026/#elgato-stream-deck)** ($100-150): Zoom mute and Home Assistant scenes.
- **[WiiM Pro](https://www.joekarlsson.com/blog/homelab-gift-ideas-2026/#wiim-pro)** ($50-150): office music, cheaper than Sonos.
- **[Aranet4 Home](https://www.joekarlsson.com/blog/homelab-gift-ideas-2026/#aranet4-home)** ($150+): my cue to open a window.
