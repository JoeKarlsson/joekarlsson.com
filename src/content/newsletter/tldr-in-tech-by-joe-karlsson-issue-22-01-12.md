[tl;dr: Agile is not achieving a sustainable speed in an optimal direction; "it is being able to change both speed and direction, and to do so easily. It is the second derivative of position with respect to time, not just the first: agility relates both to velocity and to the ability to change it, revising direction and pace as circumstances demand."](https://kevlinhenney.medium.com/agility-speed-96057078fe40)

[tl;dr: "Incentives are never perfect because management always has a limited understanding of the system it manages." Therefore, unrewarded work is often carried out to keep the system in place. [tl;dr: As a leader, you must trust your team. However, trusting your team can, at times, be at odds with helping them succeed. Human error or bad processes can come into play. Will uses "inspected trust:" when someone "brings a problem or a concern to you, trust them that there is a problem, but give yourself space to independently verify their interpretation of the problem." He highlights various ways of doing this.](https://lethain.com/inspection/)

[tl;dr: For a leader to enable her team to be more effective, 3 conditions must be met:](https://www.edbatista.com/2021/12/group-dynamics-norms-and-emotion.html)

[1) Mutual trust amongst members.](https://www.edbatista.com/2021/12/group-dynamics-norms-and-emotion.html)

[2) A group identity i.e. members belong to a unique and worthwhile group.](https://www.edbatista.com/2021/12/group-dynamics-norms-and-emotion.html)

[3) Sense of group efficacy i.e. the belief that group members are more effective working together.](https://www.edbatista.com/2021/12/group-dynamics-norms-and-emotion.html)

[At the heart of these is the ability to create awareness of emotions and regulate them, and Ed presents a framework of 14 questions and a step-by-step process that allows a group to assess where they are collectively.](https://www.edbatista.com/2021/12/group-dynamics-norms-and-emotion.html)
