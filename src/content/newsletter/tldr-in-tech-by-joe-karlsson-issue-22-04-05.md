**[tl;dr:](https://medium.com/@juanpabloaranovich/optimizing-workspace-for-productivity-focus-creativity-fcc0f28b6fa9)** [Advice around optimizing workspace includes:](https://medium.com/@juanpabloaranovich/optimizing-workspace-for-productivity-focus-creativity-fcc0f28b6fa9)

[1) Screen should be at least to nose level.](https://medium.com/@juanpabloaranovich/optimizing-workspace-for-productivity-focus-creativity-fcc0f28b6fa9)

[2) Bringing your visual to a narrow space enhances focus.](https://medium.com/@juanpabloaranovich/optimizing-workspace-for-productivity-focus-creativity-fcc0f28b6fa9)

[3) Make sure that whatever you are looking at is straight in front of your eyes.](https://medium.com/@juanpabloaranovich/optimizing-workspace-for-productivity-focus-creativity-fcc0f28b6fa9)

[4) For every 45 minutes focused on something narrow, get 5 minutes of relaxation. And more.](https://medium.com/@juanpabloaranovich/optimizing-workspace-for-productivity-focus-creativity-fcc0f28b6fa9)
