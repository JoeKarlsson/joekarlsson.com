[tl;dr: 3 steps:](https://www.edbatista.com/2022/07/how-to-deliver-bad-news.html)

[1) State what happened. The most important step is initiating the conversation. It's common to downplay bad news or share the bare minimum.](https://www.edbatista.com/2022/07/how-to-deliver-bad-news.html)

[2) Provide an explanation for the cause. This may be embarrassing, particularly if your action or inaction was a contributing factor but trying to avoid acknowledging your embarrassment often makes it worse.](https://www.edbatista.com/2022/07/how-to-deliver-bad-news.html)

[3) Here's what you're planning to do: this gives the other parties the benefit of your thinking while signaling your openness to theirs.](https://www.edbatista.com/2022/07/how-to-deliver-bad-news.html)
