[tl;dr: Lenny's framework for giving performance reviews, starts with how to prepare:](https://review.firstround.com/the-power-of-performance-reviews-use-this-system-to-become-a-better-manager)

[1) Gather direct feedback from your report & their coworkers.](https://review.firstround.com/the-power-of-performance-reviews-use-this-system-to-become-a-better-manager)

[2) Capture accomplishments.](https://review.firstround.com/the-power-of-performance-reviews-use-this-system-to-become-a-better-manager)

[3) Describe their "superpower."](https://review.firstround.com/the-power-of-performance-reviews-use-this-system-to-become-a-better-manager)

[4) Write a short high-level summary of their performance.](https://review.firstround.com/the-power-of-performance-reviews-use-this-system-to-become-a-better-manager)

[5) Identify 1-2 development areas, with examples, concrete suggestions, and what success looks like.](https://review.firstround.com/the-power-of-performance-reviews-use-this-system-to-become-a-better-manager)

[6) Share a timeline for their next career milestone. Lenny also discusses how to deliver and follow-up the review.](https://review.firstround.com/the-power-of-performance-reviews-use-this-system-to-become-a-better-manager)
