**[tl;dr:](https://bellmar.medium.com/hunting-tech-debt-via-org-charts-92df0b253145)** ["The types of problems organizations have are heavily influenced by their incentive structure." Marianne describes the types of problems you'll find in various org structures i.e. in engineering led orgs, orgs where engineering reports to product, or security, or in flat orgs. She concludes "the best organizations at managing technical debt tend to be the ones that have a thoughtful process in place to adjudicate competing incentives."](https://bellmar.medium.com/hunting-tech-debt-via-org-charts-92df0b253145)

**[tl;dr:](https://www.morling.dev/blog/whats-in-a-good-error-message/)** [Three pieces of information should be conveyed by an error message:](https://www.morling.dev/blog/whats-in-a-good-error-message/)

[1) Context: What led to the error? What was the code trying to do when it failed?](https://www.morling.dev/blog/whats-in-a-good-error-message/)

[2) The error itself: What exactly failed?](https://www.morling.dev/blog/whats-in-a-good-error-message/)

[3) Mitigation: What needs to be done in order to overcome the error?](https://www.morling.dev/blog/whats-in-a-good-error-message/)

[Gunnar also highlights best practices: having a uniform voice and style, consistency in terminology, and more.](https://www.morling.dev/blog/whats-in-a-good-error-message/)

[3) Flaky tests erode trust and breed learned helplessness.](https://abdulapopoola.com/2022/01/03/9-multipliers-for-boosting-your-teams-productivity/)
