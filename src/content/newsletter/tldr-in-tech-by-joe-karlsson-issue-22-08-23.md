[tl;dr: You want to "check that this person has internalized the feedback and will begin to make changes."](https://larahogan.me/blog/feedback-doesnt-land/)

[1) Ask them if they understand the feedback and urgency.](https://larahogan.me/blog/feedback-doesnt-land/)

[2) Intentionally create space and pauses to give them time to respond.](https://larahogan.me/blog/feedback-doesnt-land/)

[3) Ask them to reflect back what they heard.](https://larahogan.me/blog/feedback-doesnt-land/)

[4) Ask open coaching questions e.g. "okay, given this feedback, what are you planning on changing to be able to meet these expectations?"](https://larahogan.me/blog/feedback-doesnt-land/)

[5) Identify next steps.](https://larahogan.me/blog/feedback-doesnt-land/)
