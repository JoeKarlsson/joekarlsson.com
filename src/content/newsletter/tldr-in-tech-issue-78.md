## [Removing Uncertainty: The Tip Of The Iceberg](https://theengineeringmanager.substack.com/p/removing-uncertainty-the-tip-of-the)

_By: James Stanier_

tl;dr: James advises that when facing a huge, challenging project, align your team around reducing uncertainty. Focus on the most uncertain parts, get answers proving it's possible or finding another path, and repeat the process. This approach builds momentum and trust within and outside the team. ❄️🧊

## [How DoorDash Designed A Successful Write-Heavy Scalable And Reliable Inventory Platform](https://doordash.engineering/2023/02/22/how-doordash-designed-a-successful-write-heavy-scalable-and-reliable-inventory-platform/)

_By: Chuanpin Zhu, Debalin Das_

tl;dr: DoorDash's move into the Convenience and Grocery business posed a huge inventory management problem. The team built a write-heavy inventory platform to handle the scale, and this article outlines the challenges and solutions they discovered through iterations of experimentation and analysis. 🚪🛒

## [What Complex Systems Can Teach Us About Building Software](https://sookocheff.com/post/systems/what-complex-systems-can-teach-us-about-building-software/)

_By: Kevin Sookocheff_

tl;dr: Kevin Sookocheff suggests three key strategies for building successful complex software systems: (1) define and enforce quality architecture principles, (2) develop information aggregation systems for monitoring compliance, and (3) encourage experimentation and diversity in team building and software development. A thought-provoking read for software engineers.

## [Database Sharding Explained](https://architecturenotes.co/database-sharding-explained/)

_By: Mahdi Yusuf_

tl;dr: Mahdi Yusuf delves into database sharding, discussing when to use it, how to set it up, why we shard data stores, and various alternatives to sharding. This article is essential for software engineers working with databases and looking to optimize performance.

## [Common Pitfalls Of GitHub Actions](https://ashishb.net/tech/common-pitfalls-of-github-actions/)

_By: Ashish Bhatia_

tl;dr: Ashish Bhatia critiques GitHub's Actions template, pointing out its lack of dependency caching, cancelation of stale executions, path filtering, and timeouts. He also provides solutions for each issue. Software engineers working with GitHub Actions should take note of these potential pitfalls and suggested improvements.
