## [Be A Thermostat, Not A Thermometer](https://larahogan.me/blog/be-a-thermostat-not-a-thermometer/)

_By: Lara Hogan_

tl;dr: Lara explains how we can easily be influenced by others' moods and how leaders should act as thermostats, setting the temperature of the room positively. For software engineers, this means being aware of negative mood shifts and actively working to create a healthy environment.

## [How To Plan As An Engineering Executive](https://lethain.com/planning/)

_By: Will Larson_

tl;dr: Will shares valuable insights on planning as an engineering executive, including approaching planning as an infinite process, decomposing it into financial plans, functional portfolio allocations, and roadmaps. Useful for both engineers and engineering managers.

## [A Problem Vs The Problem](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-212-a-problem-vs-the-problem)

_By: John Cutler_

tl;dr: John highlights how conversations about problems and their causes are often negotiations involving identity, reputation, and control. As software engineers, understanding this can help navigate and address problems more effectively.
