**[tl;dr:](https://jacobian.org/2022/jan/31/developing-a-values-interview-question/)** [A company's core values are important to consider when hiring but, how do you develop an interview question that measures those values? Jacob outlines 3 things:](https://jacobian.org/2022/jan/31/developing-a-values-interview-question/)

[1) Describe how the value influences action.](https://jacobian.org/2022/jan/31/developing-a-values-interview-question/)

[2) Figure out what behaviors are behind the value.](https://jacobian.org/2022/jan/31/developing-a-values-interview-question/)

[3) Find a situation where this value would influence behavior.](https://jacobian.org/2022/jan/31/developing-a-values-interview-question/)
