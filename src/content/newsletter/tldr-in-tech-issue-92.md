## [The Case Against Measuring Cycle Time](https://newsletter.getdx.com/p/cycle-time)

_By: Abi Noda_

**tl;dr**: "There are cases in which individual teams may find cycle time useful. However, using cycle time as a top-level performance measure that is pushed onto all teams is counterproductive. To actually improve performance, leaders should focus on measuring the friction experienced by developers and removing the bottlenecks that slow them down." Think of cycle time like the cherry on top, useful but not the main ingredient in your productivity sundae! 🍒⏱️
