[tl;dr: Co-founder and CEO at Coda's to-do list philosophy:](https://coda.io/@shishir/to-do-list-philosophy)

[1) Start every new day fresh with no weight from the previous day's list of things to do, just a new sheet of paper with a new set of priorities, don't compound from the previous day.](https://coda.io/@shishir/to-do-list-philosophy)

[2) Do the hardest task first.](https://coda.io/@shishir/to-do-list-philosophy)

[3) Have a column with next action so you can see which tasks are missing a next step.](https://coda.io/@shishir/to-do-list-philosophy)

[4) Know whether it's on your plate or someone else's.](https://coda.io/@shishir/to-do-list-philosophy)
