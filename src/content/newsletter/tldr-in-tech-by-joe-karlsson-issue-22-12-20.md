[tl;dr: Coinbase's Director of Eng:](https://review.firstround.com/lessons-from-coinbases-wild-ascent-four-rules-for-scaling)

[1) Shape your org chart so your product reflects what you want to ship.](https://review.firstround.com/lessons-from-coinbases-wild-ascent-four-rules-for-scaling)

[2) Push part of the strategic decision-making process down the org to gain speed and flexibility.](https://review.firstround.com/lessons-from-coinbases-wild-ascent-four-rules-for-scaling)

[3) Think in 3D to visualize what's missing i.e, think about how the work gets done, not just who does it.](https://review.firstround.com/lessons-from-coinbases-wild-ascent-four-rules-for-scaling)

[4) Put people over org structures and leverage emerging leaders.](https://review.firstround.com/lessons-from-coinbases-wild-ascent-four-rules-for-scaling)
