[tl;dr: Varies on whether the company is early-stage, prioritizing growth or profitability.](https://lethain.com/should-you-prioritize-infrastructure-costs/)

[1) Early stage: If you reduce your infrastructure costs to $0, and it doesn't increase your runway by at least two months, it's not worth focusing on.](https://lethain.com/should-you-prioritize-infrastructure-costs/)

[2) Growth: "The fundamental question is whether infrastructure's share of cost of goods sold are increasing as a percentage of revenue?"](https://lethain.com/should-you-prioritize-infrastructure-costs/)

[3) Profitability: Align with the business on dollars spent on infrastructure overhead per engineer.](https://lethain.com/should-you-prioritize-infrastructure-costs/)
