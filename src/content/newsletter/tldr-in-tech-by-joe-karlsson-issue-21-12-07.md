[tl;dr: The following behaviors help engineers avoid the trap of the manager's schedule. Engineers should be:](https://github.com/readme/guides/time-management-software-engineers)

[1) Defensive of their time.](https://github.com/readme/guides/time-management-software-engineers)

[2) "Pay themselves first" - deciding how to allocate time in advance.](https://github.com/readme/guides/time-management-software-engineers)

[3) Defend the time of others.](https://github.com/readme/guides/time-management-software-engineers)

[4) Clearly designate interrupt-driven work and batch it.](https://github.com/readme/guides/time-management-software-engineers)

[5) Designate low-leverage work, and time-box it.](https://github.com/readme/guides/time-management-software-engineers)

[6) Communicate with candor.](https://github.com/readme/guides/time-management-software-engineers)

[7) Prioritize ruthlessly.](https://github.com/readme/guides/time-management-software-engineers)
