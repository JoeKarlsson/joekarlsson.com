[tl;dr: Recommendations include:](https://leerob.io/blog/developer-experience-examples)

[1) Onboard as fast as possible.](https://leerob.io/blog/developer-experience-examples)

[2) Make upgrading easy: "when making a major version change, limit the blast radius of changes to make it easy for folks to update dependencies."](https://leerob.io/blog/developer-experience-examples)

[3) Helpful error messages: "when applicable, include hyperlinks in error messages to provide more context on how to solve the error." And more.](https://leerob.io/blog/developer-experience-examples)
