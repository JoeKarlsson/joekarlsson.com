**[tl;dr:](https://hbr.org/2022/01/what-to-do-when-youre-stuck-in-the-survival-zone)** [The survival zone is when your energy is high, but negative at work. People in this zone are anxious, impatient, and self-critical. Tony shares 6 observations to avoid falling here:](https://hbr.org/2022/01/what-to-do-when-youre-stuck-in-the-survival-zone)

[1) Become a sprinter, not a marathoner.](https://hbr.org/2022/01/what-to-do-when-youre-stuck-in-the-survival-zone)

[2) Substitute self-observation for judgment.](https://hbr.org/2022/01/what-to-do-when-youre-stuck-in-the-survival-zone)

[3) When you feel bad, remember that's not the whole story.](https://hbr.org/2022/01/what-to-do-when-youre-stuck-in-the-survival-zone)

[4) Create a safe space for yourself within yourself.](https://hbr.org/2022/01/what-to-do-when-youre-stuck-in-the-survival-zone)

[5) Take up something you enjoy doing just for its own sake.](https://hbr.org/2022/01/what-to-do-when-youre-stuck-in-the-survival-zone)

[6) Make someone else's life better.](https://hbr.org/2022/01/what-to-do-when-youre-stuck-in-the-survival-zone)
