## [Directly Responsible Individuals](https://about.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/directly-responsible-individuals/)

_By: Unknown_

tl;dr: GitLab assigns a "Directly Responsible Individual" (DRI) to every project to ensure accountability for its success or failure. This article highlights the characteristics of DRIs, such as being detail-oriented, calm under pressure, and skilled at asking questions.

## [Postgres: The Graph Database You Didn't Know You Had](https://www.dylanpaulus.com/posts/postgres-is-a-graph-database)

_By: Dylan Paulus_

tl;dr: Dylan demonstrates how to store and query graph data structures in Postgres, discussing how he used this approach to dynamically generate work instructions on a manufacturing line. Postgres can be used to generate documents based on parameters and rules defined on each edge.

## [The Zen Of Proverbs](https://wagslane.dev/posts/zen-of-proverbs/)

_By: Lane Wagner_

tl;dr: This blog post provides 20 rules of thumb for writing better software, such as "optimize for simplicity first" and "reading is more important than writing." One of the most important lessons is that code should be written for humans, not computers.

## [Writing A Python SQL Engine From Scratch](https://github.com/tobymao/sqlglot/blob/main/posts/python_sql_engine.md)

_By: Toby Mao_

tl;dr: In this post, Toby Mao takes us through the process of building a Python SQL engine from scratch. He covers the steps of tokenizing, parsing, optimizing, planning, and executing, and provides a detailed explanation of each step.

## [State Of JS 2022](https://2022.stateofjs.com/en-US/)

_By: Sacha Greif, Eric Burel, Sarah Drasner_

tl;dr: The State of JS 2022 report highlights continued innovation in performance, reactivity, and hydration. It discusses how Svelte has focused on simplicity in developer ergonomics, while Solid and Qwik have explored new approaches to reactivity.

## [AI Collection](https://github.com/ai-collection/ai-collection)

## [Crane: Uber's Next-Gen Infrastructure Stack](https://www.uber.com/us/en/blog/crane-ubers-next-gen-infrastructure-stack/)

tl;dr: Uber has been on a journey to reimagine their infrastructure stack for a hybrid, multi-cloud world. In this post, they discuss the requirements they needed to satisfy, the motivation behind Crane, and key features of their implementation.

## [How eBay's Notification Platform Used Fault Injection In New Ways](https://tech.ebayinc.com/engineering/how-ebays-notification-platform-used-fault-injection-in-new-ways/)

_By: Wei Chen_

tl;dr: eBay has used fault injection in new ways to improve their notification platform. They have achieved this by injecting faults through simulation in the application level by code instrumentation, a practice which they believe to be an industry first.
