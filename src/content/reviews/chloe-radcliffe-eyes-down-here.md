I went to college with Chloe Radcliffe. She was my girlfriend's roommate.

I've followed her career ever since, and I am so goddamn proud of her. Even in college she was special. She was the absolute star of the national speech team, and she gave the speech at our graduation.

Eyes Down Here is her first hour for Comedy Central, and she's been honing this act since she was in high school, and it shows. It's a tightly built special with great jokes and great beats, and she performs it confidently, as usual. I can see pieces of the speeches she gave in college in these routines.

[Chloe Radcliffe: Eyes Down Here, the full special on Comedy Central Stand-Up's YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DW44LzeGyiI)

The birthmark on her face has been part of her act forever, as it should be, since it's the most recognizable thing about her. The title gets it out of the way before she says a word, and then she builds the hour on it.

![Chloe Radcliffe on stage in a blue top, holding the mic and smiling sideways at the crowd in front of a red velvet curtain, the round brown birthmark on her cheek in full view](/images/reviews/chloe-radcliffe-eyes-down-here/opening-birthmark-bit.webp)

It's so funny, it's so naked, it's so revealing, and it's so Chloe.

She's pretty self-deprecating about who she was in college, and hearing her give college a shout-out from the stage was so funny. Chloe has definitely had a glow-up since she moved to New York.

Comedy Central is [bringing back its hour-long specials](https://www.avclub.com/chloe-radcliff-eyes-down-here-comedy-central-announcement), and they picked the right person to help do it. If you want to see a performer who's going to keep growing and do great things, keep an eye on Chloe. She's not done with her ascension.
