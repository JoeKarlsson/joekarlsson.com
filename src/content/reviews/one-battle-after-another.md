I walked into One Battle After Another with astronomical hype - and still left the theater knowing in my gut that I'd just witnessed a five out of five star masterpiece.

This might have been the perfect movie for my girlfriend and me. We were activists in Minneapolis during the uprising after George Floyd's murder by the Minneapolis Police Department, and we're currently wrestling with what it means to bring children into this world. Watching revolutionaries grow old and inspire the next generation felt deeply personal and wildly optimistic in a way I desperately needed right now.

![Leonardo DiCaprio as Bob in a plaid robe at a kitchen table, holding a mug.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/bob-and-willa-at-the-kitchen-table-1.webp) ![Chase Infiniti as Willa across the table from him, arms crossed in a leather jacket.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/bob-and-willa-at-the-kitchen-table-2.webp)

If [Eddington](/reviews/eddington/) wallowed in nihilism and anger at the youth, which I appreciated but found limiting, this film blows it out of the water by actually believing in the possibility of change.

Paul Thomas Anderson is trying to make a populist action movie about revolutionary action wrapped around a complicated family drama, which is such a brilliant move. If you want to inspire revolution, you need to make it accessible. This movie is a little bit [Mad Max Fury Road](https://letterboxd.com/film/mad-max-fury-road/), a little bit Altman's [Nashville](https://letterboxd.com/film/nashville/), a little bit [How to Blow Up a Pipeline](/reviews/how-to-blow-up-a-pipeline-1/), a little bit [The Spook Who Sat By the Door](/reviews/the-spook-who-sat-by-the-door/) - and the best part is you don't need to have seen any of those films or even engage with the revolutionary subtext to enjoy this as pure cinema.

![Teyana Taylor and the French 75 gathered by a car at sunset.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/french-75-then-spook-1.webp 'One Battle After Another · 2025') ![Recruits in a field watching one of their own train in The Spook Who Sat by the Door.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/french-75-then-spook-2.webp 'The Spook Who Sat by the Door · 1973')

_The French 75, and The Spook Who Sat by the Door in its [4K restoration trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MQvvXatVZRg)._

I caught this in IMAX since there are no 70mm Dolby Vision theaters in the Midwest, and that final car chase sequence made me genuinely seasick as the camera rolled over waves of hills.

![A desert highway rising and dropping over hill after hill, a blue car cresting far ahead.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/road-rolls-like-waves.webp 'spoiler')

That's the power of the format and what this film demands.

Make no mistake, this is Leo's movie - but the supporting cast of Black women is absolutely phenomenal.

![Teyana Taylor as Perfidia in a cap, aiming a pistol in the dark.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/perfidia-mid-raid.webp)

Regina Hall, Junglepussy, Teyana Taylor, and Chase Infiniti are incredible.

![Regina Hall in a bandana and braids, mid-sentence in the sunset.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/regina-hall-then-junglepussy-1.webp) ![Junglepussy standing on a bank counter in a teal jacket and boots, a pistol in her outstretched hand.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/regina-hall-then-junglepussy-2.webp)

And if you told me Sean Penn had a butt plug up his ass the entire shoot as a method acting choice, I would fully believe you. His performance is completely unhinged with wild physical tics that somehow work perfectly.

![Sean Penn as Lockjaw walking down a gold hotel hallway in a suit, two men behind him.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/lockjaw-walks-the-hallway.webp)

Benicio Del Toro will probably get overshadowed, which is a shame because his character is an absolute saint, and his underground railroad is genuinely inspiring.

![Benicio del Toro as Sergio, a bag on his shoulder, leading families with kids and stuffed animals through the back of a store.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/sergio-leads-families-through-the-store.webp)

I'm an absolute sap, and I was sobbing through the final 15 minutes, which gave me exactly the catharsis I needed.

![Willa in a hooded rain jacket at dusk, checking something in her hands.](/images/reviews/one-battle-after-another/willa-heads-out.webp 'spoiler')

This movie is so emotionally complex that I'm not even going to attempt unpacking all my thoughts about the characters and their choices - but if you want to dig into that, there's so much to chew on.

This is a 5/5 masterpiece, the best film of 2025, one of the best films of all time, and an absolute blast in a real cinema. It's funny, pounding, expansive, engaging, and I fully approve and endorse every message in it. I already cannot wait to watch it again.
