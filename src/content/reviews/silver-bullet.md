Gary Busey as a drunk uncle is the best casting I've ever heard of in my entire life.

![Gary Busey as Uncle Red on a flowered couch, holding a bottle of Wild Turkey in one hand and dealing cards with the other to Corey Haim's Marty, who sits in his wheelchair in a red bathrobe, a Coke bottle on the table between them.](/images/reviews/silver-bullet/uncle-red-wild-turkey-card-game.webp)

I didn't even know he was in this. I put Silver Bullet on for Halloween to get ready for Robert Eggers' Werwulf at Christmas, expecting some werewolf dread, and got one of the funniest movies I've seen all year. Busey plays Uncle Red, drinking his way through another divorce, and he's the only adult his nephew Marty (Corey Haim), who uses a wheelchair, can go to. [A lot of Busey's ad-libs stayed in the movie](https://horrornewsnetwork.net/ten-things-you-might-not-know-about-silver-bullet/). Absolutely incredible. Dan Attias has said the gun shop scene is one where Busey ad-libbed a lot.

![Gary Busey as Uncle Red in a white hat and brown corduroy jacket, leaning on the counter of a gun shop with rifles on the wall behind him, talking to the gray-haired gunsmith in an apron.](/images/reviews/silver-bullet/uncle-red-gun-shop-lone-ranger.webp)

Seeing a reverend, a man of the cloth, try to run over a disabled kid in a wheelchair is so fucking funny, and Everett McGill plays Reverend Lowe completely straight, which makes it funnier.

![Corey Haim's Marty riding his souped-up wheelchair down a country road while a blue sedan closes in right behind him, Reverend Lowe at the wheel.](/images/reviews/silver-bullet/reverend-lowe-car-behind-marty.webp 'spoiler')

Marty's sister Jane (Megan Follows) asks, "Well, what are we gonna do?" Marty says, "I think I know," and it cuts to him writing the reverend telling him to "kill himself."

![Marty holding up a sheet of paper covered in letters cut out of newspapers and glued down in a ransom-note jumble.](/images/reviews/silver-bullet/marty-kill-yourself-note.webp 'spoiler')

I think coked-out schlock King is my favorite version of King. He wrote this the year before [Maximum Overdrive](/reviews/maximum-overdrive/), the one [he's said he was coked out of his mind for](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_Overdrive#Production).

The effects looked terrible. I loved them. King wanted a werewolf you could barely see, and the suit they ended up with looks like a black bear having a bad night. People call it the werebear.

Eggers is going to give me a werewolf accurate down to the mud, and I can't wait, But I still kind of hope he puts a gas-powered wheelchair in his version.
