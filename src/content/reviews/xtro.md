If David Lynch made E.T. for [$60,000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xtro) in a London flat, it'd look like this.

It's [Close Encounters](https://letterboxd.com/film/close-encounters-of-the-third-kind/) for horny moms and dads. Dad gets sucked up by a bright light, then comes back three years later to find his wife living with a new boyfriend.

![A father and his young son in the garden at night, shielding their eyes from a light off screen.](/images/reviews/xtro/abduction-light-then-close-encounters-1.webp 'Xtro · 1982') ![A column of white light coming down onto a country road at night.](/images/reviews/xtro/abduction-light-then-close-encounters-2.webp 'Close Encounters of the Third Kind · 1977')

_Xtro's garden abduction, next to the light on the road in Close Encounters of the Third Kind from Sony Pictures' official trailer._

![The wife on the living-room couch with her new boyfriend perched on the arm beside her, both turned toward her returned husband, who sits in the foreground facing them.](/images/reviews/xtro/dad-meets-wife-and-new-boyfriend.webp)

The standout scene has to be the birth. An alien creature impregnates a woman in a cottage, her belly balloons, and ||she gives birth to the dad, fully grown||. Jesus Christ, insane body horror.

![A grown man's face pressed to the floor, eyes squeezed shut, his face, arm and hair slick with blood and goo, a dark cord across his cheek.](/images/reviews/xtro/dad-born-again-in-goo.webp 'spoiler')

It's clear the filmmakers are earnestly taking this seriously and doing their best with what they had. It works best for me when it goes surreal: a killer clown, a life-size toy soldier, a toy tank on a kill mission.

![A clown in a yellow jacket, red bow tie and huge red shoes sits grinning on the floor in a pool of light.](/images/reviews/xtro/killer-clown.webp)

![A man-sized toy soldier with a molded plastic face, green cap and combat vest stands in a dark doorway holding a gun.](/images/reviews/xtro/life-size-toy-soldier.webp)

![A camouflaged toy tank rolls out from behind a doorframe across a wooden hallway floor and turns its gun toward the camera.](/images/reviews/xtro/toy-tank-rolls-out.webp)

It feels like a schlocky Lynchian nightmare about aliens and toys.

This isn't a good movie, don't get me wrong. But it's fast, it's so fun to watch, and it's over in 86 minutes. It's honestly a three. I'm giving it an extra star for all the blood and guts, and for not wasting my time.

Stray thoughts:

- The French au pair who gets used to hatch alien eggs is Maryam d'Abo, five years before she was a Bond girl in The Living Daylights.
- Xtro got caught in Britain's "[video nasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_nasty)" panic when it hit home video in 1983, and I'm not surprised.
