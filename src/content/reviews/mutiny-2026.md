Mutiny is [Under Siege](https://letterboxd.com/film/under-siege/) with somehow more boring, worse writing, and it's uglier to look at.

![An aerial shot down the gray deck of the battleship USS Missouri, its gun turrets cutting through rough water.](/images/reviews/mutiny-2026/under-siege-missouri-then-mutiny-cargo-deck-1.webp 'Under Siege · 1992') ![A man standing alone on top of stacked shipping containers on Mutiny's cargo ship, seen from the bridge.](/images/reviews/mutiny-2026/under-siege-missouri-then-mutiny-cargo-deck-2.webp 'Mutiny · 2026')

_The USS Missouri in the [Under Siege trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JcpyOMIUdYQ), and the cargo ship in Mutiny._

I don't put on a Jason Statham movie for the writing, though. I've followed his entire career (but mostly the fictional careers he has in every single movie: [beekeeper](/reviews/the-beekeeper-2024/), [courier](https://letterboxd.com/film/the-transporter/), [hitman](https://letterboxd.com/film/the-mechanic-2011/), [armored truck guard](https://letterboxd.com/film/wrath-of-man/), [construction foreman](https://letterboxd.com/film/a-working-man/), [deep-sea rescue diver who fights a shark](https://letterboxd.com/film/the-meg/)).

![A six-tile grid of Jason Statham at work: in a beekeeper's veil holding a honeycomb frame, driving in a black suit and leather gloves, aiming a pistol, in a Fortico Security guard uniform beside an armored truck, in a white hard hat, and in a submersible as a giant shark's open jaws fill the window](/images/reviews/mutiny-2026/statham-resume-six-jobs.webp)

_Frames from the official trailers for The Beekeeper (2024), The Transporter (2002), The Mechanic (2011), Wrath of Man (2021), A Working Man (2025) and The Meg (2018)._

In Mutiny he's head of security for a Bangkok shipping billionaire, which might be the most boring job on the résumé. The billionaire gets murdered (a billionaire???? who the fuck cares?!?!?!), Statham gets framed, and he boards a cargo ship using a dead assassin's identity.

For an action movie, this simply has too much talking. Most of it goes to the conspiracy: who wants to buy the shipping company, what the billionaire's son knew, who on the ship takes orders from whom. I put on a Statham boat movie and spent most of it hearing about a corporate takeover.

![At night, a bloodied man in a torn white shirt explains the scheme while Statham holds a pistol under his chin, with the subtitle burned in at the bottom](/images/reviews/mutiny-2026/the-company-goes-to-kameron.webp 'spoiler')

When I turn one of these on, I really expect a few more poorly choreographed fight scenes starring bald stuntmen filmed from behind. Mutiny didn't even really deliver on that. The fight with the captain near the end does give me one back-of-the-bald-head punch.

![A gray-bearded ship's captain flinching on deck as a bald man in a denim jacket, seen only from the back of his head, throws a punch at him](/images/reviews/mutiny-2026/bald-head-from-behind-in-the-captain-fight.webp 'spoiler')

I have to say, though, Jason Statham does have more rizz than Steven Seagal. He's the only thing on that ship I wanted to look at.
