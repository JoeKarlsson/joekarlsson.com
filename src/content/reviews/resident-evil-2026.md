This movie is the exact opposite of competency porn. Turns out I also love movies about an idiot NPC who stumbles through the worst night of his life.

![Austin Abrams as Bryan, a medical courier in a knit beanie, standing under a farmhouse chandelier and looking down at the pistol in his hands like it came without instructions.](/images/reviews/resident-evil-2026/bryan-fumbling-the-pistol.webp)

I went in as a guy who has [Resident Evil 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_4) in his top five games of all time. Leon Kennedy is my guy. But, Fuck Leon, because THIS MOVIE RIPS.

![Leon Kennedy, square-jawed under his blond fringe, from the Resident Evil 4 remake.](/images/reviews/resident-evil-2026/leon-kennedy-then-bryan-1.webp 'Resident Evil 4 · 2023') ![Bryan wide-eyed in his beanie in a dark farmhouse.](/images/reviews/resident-evil-2026/leon-kennedy-then-bryan-2.webp 'Resident Evil · 2026')

_Leon in Capcom's [Resident Evil 4 trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Id2EaldBaWw), and Bryan._

This isn't a faithful adaptation and it DOESNT NEED TO BE. Cregger and co-writer Shay Hatten built something that works on its own terms, whether or not you've ever picked up a controller.

[Weapons](/reviews/weapons-2025-1/) built its structure around shifting perspective across multiple characters. The problem Cregger set himself here is different: make an audience root for An incompetent idiot who lies to his girlfriend, makes openly selfish choices, kills children and babies and dogs, and still holds the screen.

![Bryan, his back to the camera in a hospital waiting room, saying "But I'm gonna be home in, like, 20 minutes" into his phone.](/images/reviews/resident-evil-2026/home-in-twenty-minutes-then-i-could-do-that-1.webp) ![Bryan facing the dispatcher a couple of minutes later, saying "I could do that."](/images/reviews/resident-evil-2026/home-in-twenty-minutes-then-i-could-do-that-2.webp)

_Bryan to his girlfriend, then Bryan when the dispatcher says the next run pays double._

Austin Abrams is why it works. He plays Bryan without making him cute about any of it, without the small self-aware winks that usually give the audience permission to keep liking a bad person. You stay with Bryan because Abrams refuses to make it easy.

![Bryan in the passenger seat of a car at night, flat-faced, with the subtitle "Can't wait around forever for her."](/images/reviews/resident-evil-2026/cant-wait-around-forever.webp)

_Bryan, about thirty seconds after Maxine disappears._

Cregger keeps finding different structural problems to solve, film by film, and still doesn't get enough credit for it as a writer.

Horror-comedies tend to drop the comedy somewhere in the second act, when the stakes arrive. Cregger never lets that happen here. The whole theater was dying (with laughter) from beginning to end, including me.

_Bryan and Paul Walter Hauser, looking for the safety._

Ninety-four minutes of consistent laughs (and scares) in a horror film is rarer than people give it credit for.

More than any Resident Evil film (or frankly any video game adaptation) before it, this one captures what it feels like to play the games: limited resources, terrible odds, locks on every doors, limited ammo, and Bryan might actually die in that dark hallway.

![A padlock on a gate in a cinderblock alley, Bryan's shoulder in the foreground, with the subtitle "What is with all the fuckin' padlocks?"](/images/reviews/resident-evil-2026/all-the-padlocks.webp)

Cregger makes you walk the farmhouse's upstairs hall at the speed you'd walk it with a controller in your hands.

![Bryan, a silhouette at the far end of a dim upstairs hallway, edging along the banister toward a door.](/images/reviews/resident-evil-2026/creeping-the-upstairs-hall.webp)

The Easter eggs are fun and completely unnecessary to enjoy the film for the newly inducted into the RE universe, which is exactly how adaptation should work. The film works independently of whether you've ever heard of Raccoon City, or ever stopped at a typewriter.

![A dark farmhouse study, cluttered with boxes and files, with a pink circle drawn around an old typewriter sitting alone in the middle of the desk.](/images/reviews/resident-evil-2026/typewriter-circled.webp)

_The study at the end of that hallway. Bryan grins at the typewriter a second later._

Stray observations:

- shoutout to the score
- as a Minnesota boy, shoutout to the snow; CGI or not, it's perfect
- shoutout to [Blank Check](https://www.blankcheckpod.com/)
- doesn't top [Barbarian](https://letterboxd.com/film/barbarian-2022/) or Weapons for me, but the most purely fun I've had in a theater in a while; already ready for a rewatch.
