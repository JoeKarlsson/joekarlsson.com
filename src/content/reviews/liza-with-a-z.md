Liza Minnelli was 26 here, and she had the whole world in her hand.

![Liza Minnelli in a white suit at a microphone, arms raised wide against a glowing red backdrop.](/images/reviews/liza-with-a-z/liza-arms-out-in-white.webp)

Fosse knows exactly what to do with Liza Minnelli. He doesn't build a TV special around her so much as get everything else out of the way and point eight cameras at her. It was shot on film at the Lyceum Theatre when every other variety special was on videotape, and you can feel the difference. It moves like a movie. The edits hit on the beat, and the dancers drift in and out like they're part of the lighting.

![Minnelli in a bowler hat and black tailcoat dances in front of a chorus line in silhouette while the backdrop shifts from lavender to red.](/images/reviews/liza-with-a-z/dancers-in-silhouette-lights-change.webp)

And then there's Liza in that red Halston dress, which deserves its own billing.

![Minnelli in a red sequined halter minidress and red tights stands on a dark stage, dancers in white shirts sitting on the floor around her.](/images/reviews/liza-with-a-z/liza-in-red-halston.webp)

It's 53 minutes long and it snaps the whole way through. You get Liza singing her face off for fifteen songs, a Cabaret medley to close it out, and the most 1972 staging you've ever seen, which I say with love.

![Minnelli in black sequins sings under a giant CABARET sign spelled out in pink light bulbs.](/images/reviews/liza-with-a-z/cabaret-sign-finale.webp)

The whole thing is a time capsule from the moment when Fosse and Kander and Ebb owned Broadway and Minnelli was the biggest star in the world.

I can't tell you exactly what she has. She's funny, a little nervous, completely in control, and 26 years old, and I couldn't take my eyes off her for a second. It was such a treat to see her right at her peak.

The negatives [went missing in the 1980s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liza_with_a_Z), and it took until 2006 for Showtime to restore it and put it back on the air. Somebody please give this a 4K restoration. It should look as good as she does.
