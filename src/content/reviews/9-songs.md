9 Songs is 70 minutes of going to a show, going home and fucking, and it's the most honest movie I've seen about being in your 20s.

The shows got me first. I was 14 when this came out, and I owned a CD by every single band in it: Black Rebel Motorcycle Club, the Von Bondies, Elbow, Primal Scream, the Dandy Warhols, Super Furry Animals, Franz Ferdinand. It's a throwback to when rock bands were huge, and we just don't have bands like this anymore. Things were so fucking cool back then, and all of these bands rip.

![Alex Kapranos sings and plays guitar in blue light, a kick drum stenciled "Franz Ferdinand" behind him.](/images/reviews/9-songs/franz-ferdinand.webp)

Yes, this movie barely has a plot. Matt (Kieran O'Brien) meets Lisa (Margo Stilley) at a Brixton Academy gig, and Michael Winterbottom just cuts back and forth between the shows they go to and the bed they go home to. It's bare bones on purpose, and after sitting with it for a day, it kind of worked for me.

Everyone gets so squeamish about sex in film. Get over it. A lot of relationships in your 20s are exactly this: maybe going out to some clubs, maybe going to a show, and fucking. The movie captures something real about that, and it couldn't without showing the sex. Stilley is pretty incredible here. Her performance is so naturalistic, and she and O'Brien have so much chemistry.

![Margo Stilley as Lisa in profile in the Brixton crowd, grinning up at the stage with her eyes half closed.](/images/reviews/9-songs/lisa-at-franz-ferdinand.webp)

It's also a movie about loneliness, even inside a relationship. The concerts look completely bootlegged, shot from inside the crowd, so you're right there in the sweat with a few thousand strangers.

![Black Rebel Motorcycle Club in silhouette under a row of white stage lights, seen over the heads of the crowd.](/images/reviews/9-songs/brmc-from-the-crowd.webp)

Then the film cuts to Matt in Antarctica, telling the story later over miles of empty ice.

![Hands clapping in silhouette over the Franz Ferdinand crowd.](/images/reviews/9-songs/crowd-then-ice-1.webp) ![The next shot after a beat of black: wind blowing snow across an empty Antarctic slope.](/images/reviews/9-songs/crowd-then-ice-2.webp)

At one point he says claustrophobia and agoraphobia live in the same place, like two people in a bed.

![Matt walks between a small orange tent and a snowmobile in a flat white expanse, with the line subtitled at the bottom.](/images/reviews/9-songs/two-people-in-a-bed.webp)

You can be lonely in a crowd of thousands. You can be lonely in your own bed, having intimate sex with somebody. Even the sad, melancholic stretches rang true to me.

It's short, sure, and it repeats the same few beats over and over. But I'm thankful this film exists. Something about it still worked for me.

Stray thoughts:

- I have so many questions about how 9 Songs was made. Did any of these bands know they were going to be in a feature film? I need to know.
- Franz Ferdinand playing "Jacqueline" is my highlight. God, I would have killed to go to that show.
