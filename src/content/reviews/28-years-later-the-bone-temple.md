Alex Garland has a lot to say about society, religion, and healing. Also there's a lot of gore.

![The Bone Temple at dusk: hundreds of pale bone-covered pillars on a hillside, lit by small flames, with a tower of skulls in the middle and a full moon over the hills.](/images/reviews/28-years-later-the-bone-temple/bone-temple-at-dusk.webp)

The Bone Temple is Nia DaCosta and Alex Garland's take on where this franchise is going, and it is such a breath of fresh air. I don't think normies are going to love it - this is weird, challenging, swerving hard in directions you don't expect. But I really did. The [needle drops](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_(Duran_Duran_song)>) are fun. The history and lore are genuinely interesting. And I love how it's less about zombies and more an exploration of how humans behave when society breaks down.

![Ralph Fiennes as Kelson, shirtless and stained orange, dancing alone among the bone pillars with his arms flung out.](/images/reviews/28-years-later-the-bone-temple/kelson-dancing-to-rio.webp)

I'll be honest - the Jimmys at the end of [28 Years Later](/reviews/28-years-later/) Part One were shocking in a bad way. I was laughing, and not with the movie. Those last two minutes did not leave me optimistic. But here, the Jimmys and the Fingers were absolutely enthralling at every step. Garland obviously has a lot to say about society, religion, healing, and whether we should punish those who hurt us or try to cure them. Spending an entire film exploring what the virus actually is and whether it can be cured? Fascinating choice.

![Samson, bearded and lying against a wall of skulls at night, looking up, with the subtitle "Moon."](/images/reviews/28-years-later-the-bone-temple/samson-says-moon.webp 'spoiler')

I didn't even realize Jimmy was played by the same guy who played Remmick in [Sinners](/reviews/sinners-2025/) this year. He's having a phenomenal year - crushed both roles completely.

![Jack O'Connell as Remmick in Sinners, grinning up out of the dark.](/images/reviews/28-years-later-the-bone-temple/remmick-then-jimmy-crystal-1.webp 'Sinners · 2025') ![Jack O'Connell as Sir Lord Jimmy Crystal, long blond hair and gold chains, scowling in a sunny field.](/images/reviews/28-years-later-the-bone-temple/remmick-then-jimmy-crystal-2.webp '28 Years Later: The Bone Temple · 2026')

_Remmick in Warner Bros.' [Sinners trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7joulECTx_U), and Jimmy Crystal._

And Ralph Fiennes is insane in this. Balls to the wall, literally naked at points, so great to see a performance this unhinged. He's a kooky weird guy but the film keeps reminding us he's a serious man of science.

![Kelson, bald and stained orange in a sleeveless vest with darts tucked in it, aiming a long blowgun at his lips.](/images/reviews/28-years-later-the-bone-temple/kelson-with-his-blowgun.webp)

Not all of it resonated with me deeply, but god damn - I'm so excited there are movies going this hard and swerving in such interesting directions. The second act turns into a [Funny Games](https://letterboxd.com/film/funny-games/) or [The Strangers](https://letterboxd.com/film/the-strangers/) style home invasion thriller, and it works. Two juggernauts fighting it out by the end. I was locked in.

![Jimmy Crystal staring into the dark, then the temple's bone pillars bursting into flame behind the silhouetted Jimmies.](/images/reviews/28-years-later-the-bone-temple/pillars-ignite-for-the-number-of-the-beast.webp 'spoiler')

_Kelson's show starts with Iron Maiden's ["The Number of the Beast"](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Number_of_the_Beast_(song)>)._

Buyer beware though - this is genuinely gory and difficult to watch at times. But damn, where this movie goes is insane.
