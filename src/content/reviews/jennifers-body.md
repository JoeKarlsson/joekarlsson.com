As a Minnesotan, I am required by law to love Diablo Cody's movies. I think she captures a Gen X view of small-town America, but I still appreciate it nonetheless.

Jennifer's Body bombed in theaters and I discovered it later in life, but I've seen it many times since. I actually showed it to my girlfriend during this viewing, which was super fun.

Devil's Kettle is in fact a real thing, and they did recently discover where it goes. Also, I'd give anything to have a Devil as our school mascot. I don't think that would happen in 2025, but a guy can dream.

The supporting cast is all incredible. I'm shouting out Amy Sedaris first - I wish she was in more things and I love whenever she pops up. Same goes for J.K. Simmons, though I know he's a frequent Diablo Cody collaborator. And of course, Amanda Seyfried and Megan Fox were incredible too.
Adam Brody feels super underrated today. Amanda is starting to get her due, but Adam is still quietly working on some incredible projects. Go watch The Kid Detective - you'll love it.

What I love about this film is the modern-day demon-possessed succubus concept hitting on the fear of rape, abuse, and assault that it instills in men - the same fear women feel on a daily basis. Honestly, we need more movies to make men afraid and lonely.

Minnesotan or not, I still recommend this movie. It's a blast. Four stars.
