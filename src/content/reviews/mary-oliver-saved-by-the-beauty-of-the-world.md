Here is a film about a woman
who taught a whole country how to kneel
in wet grass and call it prayer.
It is fine. It is a doorway,

But the thing that made me sit up
was John Waters, who should have been
ninety percent of this,
gossiping like a gull on a piling,
delighted, indiscreet, alive.

I will confess I never took her seriously.
And then a poem of hers would open a door in my chest
I did not know was a door,
and I would put on my shoes
and go out walking into the woods.
