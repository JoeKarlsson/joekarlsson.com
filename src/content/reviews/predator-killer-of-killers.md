I just love how low stakes the Predator franchise has become and the loosey goosey freedom Trachtenberg's been able to get with this franchise.

People have warm feelings towards the franchise, but it's not considered like a massive tentpole. So there's more flexibility here to make fun animated adventures, which I love. I wish there were more of these anime inspired, ambitious, dark fantasy adventure movies out there.
