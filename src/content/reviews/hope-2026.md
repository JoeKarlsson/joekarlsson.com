If you miss PS3-era texture maps, Na Hong-jin made you a 157-minute movie.

It should have been made for me, too. I'm shocked how many of the film critics I follow on Letterboxd liked it, because this is right up my alley. Hwang Jung-min plays the police chief of a little Korean harbor town, something from space shows up, and the rest is a dumb run-and-gun, cowboys-vs.-aliens movie, somewhere between [Transformers](https://letterboxd.com/film/transformers/) and [Mad Max: Fury Road](https://letterboxd.com/film/mad-max-fury-road/), with non-stop chase sequences. I love that stuff, and Hope is kind of funny. But God damn it, it looks fucking bad and it's so boring.

![Men in caps and work jackets, one with a rifle and one in a police cap and sunglasses, stand on a narrow road along a bay with hills behind.](/images/reviews/hope-2026/the-harbor-towns-shore-road-early.webp)
_The harbor town’s shore road, early in the film._

It's Fury Road if there was no character arc.

![Charlize Theron as Furiosa, head shaved and smeared with grease, leading the wives across the desert in Mad Max: Fury Road.](/images/reviews/hope-2026/fury-road-furiosa.webp) ![Hwang Jung-min, his face streaked with blood, aiming a grenade-launcher rifle out of the police car's window in Hope.](/images/reviews/hope-2026/hwang-firing-from-police-car.webp)

_Charlize Theron as Furiosa in Warner Bros.'s Fury Road trailer, and Hwang Jung-min in Hope._

The chases in Fury Road work because Furiosa and Nux come out of them different people than they went in. Nobody in Hope changes, so the handbrake turns and the gunfire turn into noise and the whole thing feels way too long.

![A sedan skids sideways down an empty mountain highway with a man dragged along its side.](/images/reviews/hope-2026/mountain-road-skid.webp)

Then there are the effects, which I'm just going to say look unforgivingly bad.

![The first creature up close in the village street: a gray, wet face half hidden under long black hair.](/images/reviews/hope-2026/first-creature-close-up.webp)

I'm not joking when I say it looks like [The Lawnmower Man](https://letterboxd.com/film/the-lawnmower-man/); that's the thought that came to my mind.

![The Lawnmower Man's gold CGI face screaming, eyes rolled up, mouth stretched open.](/images/reviews/hope-2026/lawnmower-man-face.webp) ![A close-up of one of Hope's aliens in the pine forest: a gray-green human-looking face, bloodied, with long tendrils behind it.](/images/reviews/hope-2026/forest-alien-face.webp)

_The Lawnmower Man (1992) in Scream Factory's trailer, and one of Hope's aliens (2026)._

And the commitment to rendering a whole alien invasion at this skill level is crazy.

It also has some of the most insane casting decisions I've ever seen in my entire life: ||Michael Fassbender and Alicia Vikander play the aliens in Hope||. ||They already played the most convincing robots of the 2010s||, in [Prometheus](https://letterboxd.com/film/prometheus/) and [Ex Machina](https://letterboxd.com/film/ex-machina/), ||with their own faces, and Hope covers those faces with a human-looking alien that renders like a PS3 cutscene.||

I'll keep following those critics. I just won't be taking their word on aliens anymore.
