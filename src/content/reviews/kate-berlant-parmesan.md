I don't know what to say about Kate Berlant: Parmesan, except that I laughed the whole hour.

![The special's title, Parmesan, in sparkling blackletter on black.](/images/reviews/kate-berlant-parmesan/parmesan-title-card.webp)

I didn't know much about her going in. I'd seen some of her character acting, and I had just watched her in John Early's [Maddie's Secret](/reviews/maddies-secret/), where she plays Maddie's best friend Deena. This was my first time seeing her do an hour as herself.

She's such a physical comedian.

![Kate Berlant leaning out over the front row in front of a red velvet curtain, one arm stretched flat toward the crowd.](/images/reviews/kate-berlant-parmesan/berlant-full-body-gesture.webp)

Berlant really takes advantage of the physicality of her body in this special, and a lot of the laughs come from what her body is doing while she talks.

![Kate Berlant moving around the stage in a wide shot, arms going the whole time.](/images/reviews/kate-berlant-parmesan/berlant-stage-loop.webp)

It's a very funny comedy special. I loved it. I hope to see more from her, and it has me wanting to watch more women doing stand-up.
