The onslaught in Onslaught shows up late and leaves early.

Adam Wingard and Simon Barrett are back with a great B movie setup: a desert trailer park, a former Army sniper (Adria Arjona) raising her daughter, and a squad of genetically engineered super soldiers who break out of the secret government lab next door. Wingard clearly has [Assault on Precinct 13](/reviews/assault-on-precinct-13/) on the brain.

![A rifle firing out of a police station window in a burst of smoke in Assault on Precinct 13.](/images/reviews/onslaught-2026/precinct-13-window-then-trailer-window-1.webp 'Assault on Precinct 13 · 1976') ![Adria Arjona aiming a rifle out of a dark trailer window with an older man crouched beside her.](/images/reviews/onslaught-2026/precinct-13-window-then-trailer-window-2.webp 'Onslaught · 2026')

_A rifle out the station window in the American Genre Film Archive's trailer for Assault on Precinct 13, and Arjona's trailer here._

Carpenter's siege happens through a police station's windows. Wingard's happens through a trailer's. And casting Michael Biehn is a nice treat for anyone who grew up renting [The Terminator](/reviews/the-terminator/) and Aliens.

![Michael Biehn, gray-haired in a dark jacket, standing in a dim lab corridor with an armed guard behind him.](/images/reviews/onslaught-2026/michael-biehn-first-scene.webp)

And it looks fantastic. The night exteriors are the best thing in the movie, with the fog machines running at full blast and the trailer park lit up in that hazy, colored 1980s glow.

![Mobile homes at night under two bright lights in the sky, blue fog drifting over their roofs.](/images/reviews/onslaught-2026/trailer-park-fog-at-night.webp)

Wingard has said this was a cheap shoot by today's standards. You'd never know it from the frame.

![A string of bulbs hangs over the trailer park at night while fog rolls past the mobile homes behind it.](/images/reviews/onslaught-2026/trailer-park-fog-rolling.webp)

The problem is everything between the setup and the payoff. Onslaught runs 92 minutes and still feels padded, which is a rare trick for an action movie this short. The plotting is weird, a lot of the middle feels like stalling, and by the time the super soldiers actually lay siege to the place, it's over almost as soon as it starts.

![Three super soldiers silhouetted in blue fog beside a trailer.](/images/reviews/onslaught-2026/super-soldiers-arrive-then-siege-ends-1.webp 'spoiler') ![A mother and her crying daughter holding each other after the fight.](/images/reviews/onslaught-2026/super-soldiers-arrive-then-siege-ends-2.webp 'spoiler')

The siege is also where the acting gets shaky, which hurts, since that's the part I showed up for.

There are a couple of moments in there that worked for me. Not enough of them.

My expectations going in were pretty low, and Onslaught still didn't quite clear them. I doubt I'll revisit it. But I had a mostly fun time, and I can see it becoming the kind of B movie people stumble onto on a streaming service in five years and talk up to their friends. It's a scrappy little 1980s throwback with great fog, and some nights that's enough.
