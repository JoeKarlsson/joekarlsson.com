I really wanted to love this movie. However, I was not able to get this movie to live up to the hype in my head. I know there was some changes in directors. But it just didn't have the whimsy of the first two Paddington films. Also, I was planning on seeing this in the theater and missed its run before it went to streaming. I was going to give them money for this movie, but they blew it.
