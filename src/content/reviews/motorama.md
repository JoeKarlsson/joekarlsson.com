Answers the age-old question of what would happen if you combined The Adventures of Pete and Pete and Pee-wee's Big Adventure and made it rated R. The answer is, you'd get Motorama. What a crazy movie. I loved it.

There's a little bit of Repo Man in here too - that late stage capitalist messaging about the endless rat race faceless corporations make us run just to keep us entertained and empty. The whole thing is a gas station contest gone fever dream, a kid driving across America chasing prizes that don't mean anything.

It's absurdist and wild, a twisted reflection on the American road movie. The West here isn't freedom - it's an endless loop of branded experiences and hollow rewards. And somehow it wraps all of that in this surreal, almost children's-show aesthetic that makes the darkness hit harder.

How did this movie get made? I'm so glad it did.
