Max Cady cackling through Problem Child with a lit cigar is the worst movie date ever filmed.

![Robert De Niro as Max Cady slumped in a dark movie theater seat in a loud printed shirt, head thrown back, mouth wide open, a cigar in his hand and smoke drifting through the projector beam.](/images/reviews/cape-fear-1991/cady-cackling-through-problem-child.webp)

This was the movie I was too scared to watch as a kid. I remember picking it up at Blockbuster, staring at the box and deciding it was too adult for me. I put it back and never picked it up again. Now I'm back on my [Scorsese kick](/reviews/the-color-of-money/), and I finally watched it.

It's not my favorite Scorsese, but it's the one where he seems to be having the most fun. He's playing with Hitchcock and Brian De Palma here, all the way down to [Bernard Herrmann's score from the 1962 original, reworked by Elmer Bernstein](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Fear_(1991_film)#Style>). Even the opening titles come from [Saul and Elaine Bass](/reviews/the-age-of-innocence/), and Saul made Hitchcock's titles for Vertigo and Psycho.

![Dark water ripples in streaks of brown and gold behind the opening credit for the title sequence designers.](/images/reviews/cape-fear-1991/elaine-and-saul-bass-titles.webp)

The camera work was a bit distracting, honestly, but I didn't mind too much. It feels like him playing around more than usual, and I had fun playing along. Some scenes even fade out through solid red.

![Jessica Lange with her eyes closed and a hand on her cheek, the whole frame washing into deep red as the scene fades out.](/images/reviews/cape-fear-1991/leigh-fading-to-red.webp)

And Robert De Niro is the pulp. Max Cady is tattooed, Bible-quoting and absolutely unhinged, and De Niro plays him as an amazing psychopath who's having a better time than anyone else on screen.

![Seen through cell bars, Cady stands shirtless with his back to the camera, a huge cross tattooed down his spine with the scales of justice hanging from its arms and the words truth and justice underneath.](/images/reviews/cape-fear-1991/cady-back-tattoos-prison-release.webp)

![Cady in a white cap and a loud print shirt, blurred in the foreground, talks at Nick Nolte's lawyer, who stands in a gray suit and glasses outside a discount store and stares back.](/images/reviews/cape-fear-1991/cady-quotes-galatians.webp)

He's never scarier than when he's laughing.

I wish they still made adult dramas like this. Big stars, a big studio, sex and menace and a family coming apart, made for grown-ups.

![Juliette Lewis as Danielle, smiling and holding her schoolbooks on a candy-cane stage set, while Cady sits in a doorway across the stage watching her.](/images/reviews/cape-fear-1991/cady-and-danielle-school-theater.webp)

I think if I'd watched this when I was nine it would have felt like the smartest, most adult film of all time. It probably would have been. Would I have been scared? Maybe a little. This time I just had fun.

Stray thoughts:
