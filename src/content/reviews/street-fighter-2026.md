I recently rewatched the [1995 Mortal Kombat](/reviews/mortal-kombat/), and it made me feel things. Street Fighter made me feel nothing.

Mortal Kombat at least gives its fighters some character development, so you care who's fighting. When Liu Kang fights and Orbital's "[Halcyon + On + On](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halcyon_(Orbital_song)>)" kicks in, I want to run around the house and scream.

![Robin Shou as Liu Kang, shirtless and mid-fight in a dark, smoky temple.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/liu-kang-mortal-kombat.webp 'Mortal Kombat · 1995')
![Ken in his red gi trading blows with Dhalsim in a sunlit arena packed with fans.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/ken-vs-dhalsim.webp 'Street Fighter · 2026')
_Liu Kang in the 1995 Mortal Kombat (from a YouTube upload), then Ken and Dhalsim in Street Fighter's tournament ring._

Street Fighter swaps that out for fan service and celebrity cameos, down to the Philippou brothers playing themselves, and I came out bored and feeling dumb. Andrew Koji's Ryu is a monk-like fighter who barely emotes, which doesn't give you much to work with, and Noah Centineo's Ken arc did nothing for me either. The 1994 Street Fighter movie had the same problem. It's crazy we haven't learned that lesson yet.

![Eric André in a shiny silver suit shouting into a stars-and-stripes megaphone, framed in an old TV set in front of a Combat News logo.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/eric-andre-combat-news.webp)

You'd think a movie called Street Fighter would have better street fighting in it.

Most of it is talking and shitty comedy, which tracks for [Kitao Sakurai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitao_Sakurai), who directed Bad Trip and The Eric Andre Show. When the fights finally show up, the wirework and CGI look so fake and bad.

![Ken leaping across the tournament ring at Dhalsim under shafts of stage light, the crowd on its feet behind them.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/arena-flying-kick.webp)

Half the cast looks like they were filmed on a blue screen in another room.

![Eric André as Don Sauvage, in a sequined jacket and bow tie, leaning in to talk to a man with a long ponytail, a soft-focus wall of crowd behind them.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/eric-andre-arena-closeup.webp)

The crowd shots look like individually filmed actors transposed in, exactly like the final basketball game in Space Jam: A New Legacy, and I don't mean that as a compliment.

![A wide shot of Street Fighter's arena, a dark sea of fans around the lit ring.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/street-fighter-crowd.webp 'Street Fighter · 2026')
![Space Jam: A New Legacy's stands packed with costumed Warner Bros. characters, flying monkeys, Pennywise and Don Cheadle in a red sweater.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/space-jam-crowd.webp 'Space Jam: A New Legacy · 2021')
_Street Fighter's tournament crowd, then the stands at Space Jam: A New Legacy's final game (from a YouTube clip)._

If I were directing this, I'd at least focus on the fight choreo. I'm not a big fan of the [new Mortal Kombat movies](/reviews/mortal-kombat-ii/) either, but their fight choreo is fine. Here it felt uninspired. I went in with low expectations and still didn't love it.

Stray thoughts:

- This should have been catnip for me. I'm a 90s kid, the movie is set in 1993, and I like the 90s soundtrack. None of the 90s stuff did anything for me, and I wish it had.

![A faded snapshot of young Ryu in a denim jacket and Ken in a neon windbreaker, both throwing peace signs in front of Tokyo Tower.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/ryu-ken-tokyo-photo.webp)

- Cody Rhodes is actually pretty good as Guile.

![Cody Rhodes as Guile, flattop and dog tags, grinning in a hotel hallway.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/cody-rhodes-guile.webp)

- David Dastmalchian is okay as M. Bison. I just love that actor.

![David Dastmalchian as M. Bison in his peaked cap and red uniform, lit from one side in a dark room.](/images/reviews/street-fighter-2026/david-dastmalchian-bison.webp)

- Jason Momoa as Blanka and 50 Cent as Balrog give the worst performances in it, and Momoa also produced it.
- Flagrant podcast host Andrew Schulz plays Dan Hibiki, and he sucks.
