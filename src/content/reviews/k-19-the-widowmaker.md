This movie was boring and slightly derivative (they basically did the ending of Schindler's list here), but I do commend Bigelow for creating a big budget. Hollywood film about the heroes of the Soviet Union in 2002. That's impressive and I commend her for that. However, it's hard to get past it being a boring slog.
