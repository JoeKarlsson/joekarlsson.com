The theater was packed. I can't wait to see what normies think of this.

I went into this movie with massive hype and somehow it still totally worked for me.

I love a story about a hustler. An unlikable shyster doing anything it takes to get ahead, failing at every step along the way. This feels like a deeply American film and a deeply Jewish film to me. There's something in the Moses name I haven't fully unpacked yet - I know it's saying something important, I just don't know what.

![Marty, arms folded in a short-sleeved knit shirt, while Wally, his back to the camera, peels off cash in a crowded bowling alley.](/images/reviews/marty-supreme/marty-and-wally-hustle-the-bowlers.webp)

Timothée Chalamet gives an unbelievable performance. Perfectly tuned, threading the needle of playing a lying asshole you're still rooting for just a tiny bit. It's so funny to me that this feels like Timmy talking about his own acting in real life - if you've seen any of his Oscar press tours the last couple years, you know exactly what I mean. Chalamet [says](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/timothee-chalamet-marty-supreme-press-tour-comments/) those press tour antics are "in the spirit of Marty." Currently he's number two in my Best Actor power rankings behind Leo, but I wouldn't be disappointed if he won.

The supporting cast is incredible. Fran Drescher, Teller, Mr. Wonderful, Odessa A'zion, and especially Gwyneth Paltrow. I'd kind of written her off since she hasn't done much lately, but she's absolutely incredible here. Welcome back.

![Gwyneth Paltrow as Kay Stone in glasses and a robe, on the phone by green velvet curtains in her hotel suite.](/images/reviews/marty-supreme/kay-stone-at-the-ritz.webp)

The movie is a bit long - there's a perfect 90-minute or two-hour version in there. It drags slightly at the end of the second act before the third act absolutely nails the landing. The dog stuff felt like it could have been trimmed, though I know it gives good character development for Odessa's character.

The soundtrack and score are great. I need to look up [who did this](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oneohtrix_Point_Never), but it felt very Oneohtrix Point Never inspired - the Safdie collaborator on [Good Time](https://letterboxd.com/film/good-time/) and [Uncut Gems](/reviews/uncut-gems/). That sonic anxiety is back.

It's horny, sexy, confident and weird.

![A bathtub crashing through a ceiling in a burst of plaster, then Marty, soaked and knees up, clutching his head in the tub as it lands.](/images/reviews/marty-supreme/bathtub-crashes-through-the-floor.webp 'spoiler')

I teared up at the end, ||watching Marty look at his child||. That hit me hard. I don't think much is going to change for him. I don't think he'll be happier. But [that needle drop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everybody_Wants_to_Rule_the_World) was perfectly executed.
