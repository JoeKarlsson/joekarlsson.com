Watching Blow-Up is like watching an idiot try to solve a crime. It's [The Big Lebowski](/reviews/the-big-lebowski/), but not funny.

For the first hour I wasn't sure who the idiot was: the photographer or the director. I'd never seen a Michelangelo Antonioni film, and this one is distracted and unfocused, wandering away from its own plot every ten minutes. It turns out that's extremely intentional. He did the same thing in [L'Avventura](https://letterboxd.com/film/lavventura/), which loses a woman and then stops looking for her.

David Hemmings plays Thomas, a London fashion photographer who snaps a couple in a park, blows up the prints, and finds what looks like a body in the grass. Then he can't stay interested in the murder.

Thomas is horrible. He barks at his models, rolls around his studio with two more who want a shoot, buys a giant propeller on a whim, and goes to a party to get a friend to come see the body and just gets stoned. Every step along the way, he's filling the void. Vanessa Redgrave takes her top off to get the film back, and he still hands her the wrong roll. The photos are hotter to him than she is.

_Thomas lines up his models in the studio._

![A woman in a blue skirt holds a tall wooden aircraft propeller upright on a London street while David Hemmings opens the door of a black Rolls-Royce convertible.](/images/reviews/blow-up/the-propeller-carried-out-to-his.webp)
_The propeller, carried out to his convertible._

They're the one thing he can't put down.

![The camera drifts across black-and-white prints pinned to Thomas's studio wall, from a woman in a checked shirt holding up her hand to the same woman pulling at a man in the park.](/images/reviews/blow-up/prints-on-the-wall.webp)

He stares deeper and deeper into the grain, and the closer he gets, the less there is.

![Three frames: Thomas's photo of a couple embracing under the trees in an empty park, a grainy blow-up of the bushes with a pale shape lying in the grass, and a blow-up enlarged so far it is only black and white blotches.](/images/reviews/blow-up/park-photo-to-grain.webp)

His heart ultimately finds nothing. This is [The Conversation](https://letterboxd.com/film/the-conversation/) for pictures instead of sound, with a way less gratifying conclusion. Gene Hackman at least gets a twist.

||At the end, mimes play tennis without a ball.|| ||Thomas picks up the ball that isn't there and throws it back||. I spent two hours doing the same thing with this movie, and I'm glad I played along.

![Thomas, in a dark jacket and white trousers, stoops in an empty park to pick up a tennis ball that isn't there, then winds up and throws it back overhand.](/images/reviews/blow-up/invisible-tennis-ball.webp 'spoiler')

Stray thoughts:

- [The Yardbirds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Yardbirds) playing "Stroll On" in Blow-Up was so fun, with Jeff Beck and Jimmy Page on the same stage.
