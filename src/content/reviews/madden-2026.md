Madden is a bad SNL sketch about John Madden that runs almost two hours and has nothing to say besides being a Wikipedia summary of a man.

A sketch about a sport I don't even follow. I'm not a football guy, but a sports biopic can still get me. [Miracle](/reviews/miracle/) did, and I never got hockey either. Madden has everything it needs to pull that off: Nicolas Cage as Madden, Christian Bale as Raiders owner Al Davis, David O. Russell directing.

My biggest problem is that it tries to cover a ton, and it does it poorly. The coaching years, the Super Bowl, the broadcast booth, the video game, his marriage, Al Davis's marriage. The screenplay just says and then, and then, and then, and then, with zero connection between any of it. Miracle gets away with being by the numbers because it's one season building to one game, while Madden covers forty years and never builds to anything. I know it's all real. That doesn't make it a story, and none of it pays off.

It plays like twenty sketches stapled together, each with its own shitty wig.

![Christian Bale as Al Davis, in tinted glasses and a leather jacket, walks up a driveway beside a woman in an enormous blonde curly wig and big sunglasses.](/images/reviews/madden-2026/davis-arrival-wigs.webp)
_Al Davis arrives, and so does the wig._

It cuts around trying so hard to be a fun popcorn movie, skipping ahead before anything has time to matter, and the editing choices are just weird. Bad impressions, over-the-top performances, way too much stuffed in. It's crazy.

Christian Bale is giving an insanely incredible performance in the middle of all this.

![Christian Bale as Al Davis, slicked-back hair and tinted glasses, throws both hands up mid-rant while Nicolas Cage's Madden, in a teal windbreaker, looks away.](/images/reviews/madden-2026/bale-al-davis-rant.webp)
_Bale's Al Davis, mid-rant._

Nick Cage is doing a caricature of Madden that never once feels like a real person. I did find Cage amusing at some points, but Jesus Christ.

![Nicolas Cage in Madden prosthetics and a headset gestures in the broadcast booth next to Joel Murray as Pat Summerall.](/images/reviews/madden-2026/cage-madden-booth.webp)
_Cage's Madden in the booth with Joel Murray's Pat Summerall._

Every breakup, every hospital vigil, every win, every loss, and I felt literally nothing in my heart. I never smiled. This movie should have worked. Maybe it'll do gangbusters streaming numbers for Amazon over Thanksgiving.

![A hand reaches straight into a turducken on a platter.](/images/reviews/madden-2026/turducken-hand.webp)
_The turducken, by hand._

But this was not for me, and I can't recommend it to anybody. Miracle got me anyway. Madden never did.
